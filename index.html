//Process

A Simple, Proven Build Process

Four steps, a written quote before anything starts, and no surprise variation orders halfway through.

Request your free quote

Call, WhatsApp or send the form. We will ask what you want done, what your budget looks like, and when you need it finished.

Site assessment and plan

We come out, measure up, check the structure and drainage, and go back with a written quote that breaks down materials and labour.

Build and supervision

A named site supervisor runs the job. You get a weekly photo update and a call if anything on the programme changes.

Handover and warranty

We walk the job with you, clear the defect list, hand over the as-built details, and back the workmanship for 12 months.

No. The site visit, measure-up and written quote are free anywhere in the Klang Valley. If the job needs a structural engineer's input before we can price it, we will say so and tell you what that costs before anything is commissioned.

As a rough guide: a bathroom strip-out and rebuild runs RM 18,000 to RM 35,000, a wet and dry kitchen reconfiguration RM 45,000 to RM 90,000, and a rear double-storey extension RM 160,000 upward depending on span and finishes. Your written quote will be specific; these are starting points, not a price list.

For works that need it, yes — we prepare the drawings and support the submission to MBPJ, MBSA, DBKL or MPK. Approval timelines are the council's, not ours, so we build that waiting period into the programme rather than pretending it does not exist.

External works slow down between October and March. We programme roofing, external render and drainage outside the worst of it where we can, and we tell you up front which parts of your job are weather-dependent.
